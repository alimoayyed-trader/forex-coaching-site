FOREX BASICS

What Is Forex?

Forex, short for foreign exchange, is the global market where currencies are exchanged. This guide explains how forex works and the basic concepts beginners need to understand.

BEGINNER GUIDE

What Is Forex?

Forex, or foreign exchange, refers to the buying and selling of one currency against another. Unlike a stock exchange with one central marketplace, the global forex market operates through a network of financial institutions, banks, brokers and other participants.

How Does Forex Work?

Forex is traded in currency pairs. When you trade a pair, you are comparing the value of one currency with another. The first currency is the base currency and the second is the quote currency.

What Is a Currency Pair?

Base Currency

The base currency is the first currency in a pair.

Quote Currency

The quote currency is the second currency and shows how much is needed to express the value of one unit of the base currency.

Example: EUR/USD

In EUR/USD, EUR is the base currency and USD is the quote currency. A price for EUR/USD therefore expresses the value of the euro in US dollars.

What Are Pips and Spreads?

A pip is a commonly used unit for measuring a small change in a forex exchange rate. The spread is the difference between the bid price and the ask price. These concepts help traders understand price movement and trading costs.

What Is a Forex Broker?

A forex broker is a company that provides access to trading markets and related services. Broker conditions can differ, so beginners should understand fees, execution, regulation and risk before choosing one.

What Are the Risks of Forex Trading?

Forex trading involves financial risk. Leverage can increase the size of both potential gains and losses relative to the money committed. Beginners should learn risk management before trading with real money.

What Should You Learn Next?
